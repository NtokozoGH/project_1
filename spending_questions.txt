Spending Analysis Practice Questions
=====================================
Use my_spending.csv (columns: date, category, amount) to answer these.

BASICS
------
1. What's the total amount spent across the whole file (all months)?
2. How many transactions are there in total?
3. What's the average amount spent per transaction?

MONTHLY ANALYSIS
-----------------
4. How much was spent in September 2026?
5. Which single day had the highest total spend?
6. What's the average daily spend for the month?

CATEGORY ANALYSIS
------------------
7. Which category had the highest total spend?
8. Which category had the most transactions (not necessarily the highest amount)?
9. What percentage of total spend does "Groceries" make up?
10. Sort all categories from highest to lowest spend.

SLIGHTLY HARDER
----------------
11. Find the single largest transaction (amount, category, and date).
12. List all transactions above R300.
13. Group spend by week instead of by category - which week was the most expensive?
14. If you had a monthly budget of R3,500, which categories would you need to cut to get under budget?
15. Add a new transaction to the CSV programmatically (append a row) and confirm the total updates.
